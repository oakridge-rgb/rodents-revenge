How to Play

Goal: trap every cat by pushing the green blocks so each cat is blocked in on all eight sides. A pinned cat turns into cheese. When every cat on the board is pinned at once, the round is clear — the clock winds up and releases the next batch of cats. Survive all spawn waves to advance to the next level.

You: walk with the arrow keys / WASD (swipe or D-pad on touch screens). Push single blocks or whole chains. Chain ends blocked by a wall, trap or yarn cannot be pushed; a cat at the end of the chain flees if it can.

Dangers: cats eat you on contact (3 lives). Sink holes swallow you for 10 seconds while cats keep moving. Mouse traps kill instantly. Yarn balls fly diagonally and explode against walls and blocks.

Clock: the stopwatch in the status bar sweeps once every 30 seconds. When its minute hand reaches the blue line, more cats arrive and the line moves further out.

Scoring: cheese +100 · pinned cat +1 · +1 for every 15 seconds survived. Score carries across levels; high scores are kept on this device.

About Rodent's Revenge

A faithful fan port of Rodent's Revenge (Microsoft, 1991, designed by Christopher Lee Fraley) for modern browsers and Android, rebuilt from the original's documented mechanics, reverse-engineered level data and gameplay footage.

No original Microsoft code or artwork is used. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft. See the repository's FAITHFULNESS.md for what is authentic, what is reconstructed, and what remains uncertain.
